## Abstract

This notebook examines the transition from prototype to conformity for bamboo construction components, particularly when culms are conditioned, coated, filled, or joined to polymers. The review compares Brazilian and international documents on structural design, culm characterization and grading, engineered bamboo products, building performance, reaction-to-fire testing, technical assessment of innovative products, and professional responsibility. No single standard can qualify the complete hybrid system. Conformity must be assembled across layers — feedstock, process, interface, component, connection, subsystem, building, and production — and linked to a defined application and exposure class. ISO 22156:2021 excludes engineered bamboo products and reinforced materials in which bamboo is not the primary load-bearing constituent; engineered-product test standards do not automatically cover filled natural culms; certificates for isolated polymers do not classify the finished component. In Brazil, innovative construction products without sufficient prescriptive coverage may require performance assessment through SiNAT, including testing and production-control audits. The notebook proposes an application–requirement–document–test–evidence–responsibility matrix and eight release gates. Standardization is treated as a decision architecture in which every claim identifies its object, method, version, boundary, and accountable professional.

**Keywords:** structural bamboo; standardization; conformity; testing; innovative product; SiNAT; production control; professional responsibility.

## 1. Introduction

The first three notebooks formed a clear progression. Notebook 1 examined socioecological preservation; Notebook 2 followed conditioned bamboo up to the protected component; and Notebook 3 examined the conditions for converting components and equipment into territorial productive capacity. Notebook 4 introduces a question that cuts across all of them:

> **Which documents, tests, responsibilities, and controls permit releasing a material, component, or system for a defined application?**

Standards do not validate abstract ideas. They delineate objects, conditions, methods, sampling, criteria, and responsibilities. A test standard can produce data without establishing acceptance. A design standard can require characteristic properties obtained through another document. A certificate for a sample can be authentic and still be irrelevant to the built component.

The system examined adds a difficulty: natural culm, chemical conditioning, bio-based PU, internal foam, coating, connection, and final geometry belong to different documentary domains. The hybrid component does not automatically inherit the conformity of each input.

**Cross-notebook articulation.** Notebook 2 detailed the conditioning protocols and proposed an integrated interface-cycle logic. Notebook 3 situated territorial productive capacity as a precondition for scale. The present notebook addresses the layer of regularization and responsibility that precedes and follows them: without it, prototype and production remain disconnected from formal housing use. Notebook 5 will take up the experience of dwelling, and Notebook 7 will integrate the full cycle within the context of planetary crisis. Reading this notebook presupposes Notebook 2 for the testing protocols and Notebook 3 for the criteria of productive capacity.

## 2. Authorial perspective and principles of delimitation

### 2.1. Conformity is a chain of demonstrations

In the **Universo Takwara**, rigor should not appear only at the end, when a report is sought. It must organize from the very first test specimen:

- what the object is and its version;
- for which function and exposure it was conceived;
- which materials and processes constitute it;
- what failures may occur;
- which document offers a pertinent method;
- who measures, who interprets, and who answers;
- which evidence allows moving forward;
- what requires returning to the design.

Each advance changes the state of the claim. "Built," "tested," "characterized," "met the criterion," "technically assessed," and "released for use" are not equivalent.

### 2.2. The object comes before the standard

Choosing a standard by the name of the material produces false coverage. Before the normative search, one must define:

- whole culm, lamina, panel, connection, column, wall, or building;
- structural function, enclosure, finish, filling, insulation, or protection;
- residential, institutional, industrial, temporary, or experimental use;
- indoor, protected outdoor, exposure to water, soil, radiation, or fire;
- service life, maintenance, inspectability, and consequence of failure;
- one-off fabrication, pilot batch, or repetitive production.

The same test specimen can answer a research question and be insufficient for a design decision.

### 2.3. A technical document does not transfer conformity

Product data sheet, internal report, batch certificate, scientific article, test standard, design standard, and technical assessment have different functions. None of them should receive authority that the document itself does not claim.

A commercial declaration can guide processing. An article can demonstrate behavior under delimited conditions. A standard can prescribe a method. A laboratory can issue a result. The decision to use depends on the relationship between these records and on the competent technical responsibility.

## 3. Method

### 3.1. Design

A repository-guided normative documentary review was carried out, complemented by consultation of the official catalogs of ISO, ABNT, and the Ministry of Cities portal. Priority was given to:

- documents directly related to structural culms;
- standards for engineered bamboo products;
- reaction-to-fire methods pertinent to products;
- the Brazilian framework for assessing innovative construction systems;
- legislation on technical responsibility;
- full-text academic sources analyzing standardization and connections.

Protected full-text standards are not redistributed. The text uses title, edition, status, public scope, and the documentary relationships needed for the argument.

### 3.2. Cutoff date and validity control

The consultation was closed on **July 20, 2026**. The status of a standard is temporal. Before any protocol, contract, or design, a new consultation with the issuing body must be carried out to check for revision, amendment, errata, replacement, or withdrawal.

### 3.3. Precedence rule

For each application, the route must identify:

1. mandatory legislation and regulations;
2. applicable Brazilian standards and their current editions;
3. state, municipal, and Fire Department requirements;
4. international or foreign documents accepted to fill a gap;
5. technical assessment guideline for innovative product, when pertinent;
6. project specification, test plan, and production control;
7. professional responsibility documentation.

An international standard does not automatically replace a Brazilian standard or a legal requirement. It can provide method, comparison, or technical basis when its adoption is justified and accepted by the responsible parties and pertinent authorities.

### 3.4. Classes of documents

| Class | Function | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Design** | establishes calculation, safety, and service bases | depends on properties, actions, scope, and responsible professional |
| **Testing** | defines how to produce a measurement | may not establish approval or design value |
| **Grading** | organizes material or product into classes | does not demonstrate whole-building performance |
| **Product** | fixes manufacturing and supply requirements | applies only to the covered product |
| **Performance** | assesses behavior in use | depends on application, configuration, and criteria |
| **Technical assessment** | organizes evidence for innovative solution | specific to product, holder, process, and validity |
| **Certificate or report** | records result for sample or object | does not transfer to another formulation, batch, or geometry |
| **Responsibility** | identifies professional and assumed scope | does not replace testing nor extend legal attributions |

## 4. Normative map of bamboo

### 4.1. Brazilian base identified

The **ABNT NBR 16828:2020 — Bamboo structures** series constitutes the directly identified Brazilian reference for the topic:

- **Part 1 — Structural design**;
- **Part 2 — Determination of physical and mechanical properties of bamboo**.

[Marçal (2018)](https://takwaratec.github.io/acervo-soberania-tecnologica/analyses/bambu-estrutural/marcal-2018-normas-internacionais-bambu/), prior to the series publication, already demonstrated that design, species, supply chain, maturity, selection, preservation, drying, execution, and professional knowledge form an inseparable set. The subsequent Brazilian standard does not make that chain unnecessary; it offers a national reference for structuring it.

The full current text must be purchased or consulted through the authorized channels of [ABNT](https://www.abntcatalogo.com.br/). The notebook does not assume that every component containing bamboo is automatically covered. Filled, coated, or polymer-associated culms must be confronted with the scope and limitations of the applicable edition.

### 4.2. ISO 22156:2021 — design with culms

[ISO 22156:2021](https://www.iso.org/standard/73831.html) applies to structures whose main resistance is provided by round bamboo or to shear-wall panel systems with culm frames. Except for the exception indicated in the document itself, its declared scope covers small residential, commercial, or institutional buildings and light industrial ones, one or two storeys and up to 7 m in height.

The document addresses mechanical resistance, serviceability, and durability. It does not cover thermal or acoustic insulation. Its public scope excludes:

- engineered products such as glued laminated bamboo, cross-laminated bamboo, oriented strand panels, and densified materials;
- reinforced materials in which bamboo is not the main load-bearing constituent;
- bamboo scaffolding.

In April 2026, the edition entered systematic review. It remains published, but its status must be checked before use. For the authorial system, the decisive point is that filling a culm with foam or turning it into a hybrid does not allow assuming automatically that all design rules remain applicable without additional qualification.

### 4.3. ISO 22157:2019 — culm characterization

[ISO 22157:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/65950.html) specifies methods for test specimens obtained from round culms and allows determining physical and mechanical properties intended for design or research. The public scope includes moisture content, density, mass per length, and tensile, compressive, and bending properties in defined directions.

The official catalog records the edition as published and indicates a revision in development. The document produces properties of the tested bamboo. By itself, it does not characterize the bamboo–PU interface, the foam, the coating, or the hybrid component after processing.

### 4.4. ISO 19624:2018 — culm grading

[ISO 19624:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/65528.html) establishes principles and procedures for visual and mechanical grading of culms for structural use. Its public scope informs that:

- visual grading is based on observable characteristics;
- mechanical grading uses non-destructive measurements correlated to characteristic values;
- the procedures can integrate an acceptance regime;
- the document does not define acceptance criteria;
- bamboo must be graded in the dry or stabilized state provided by the document.

The edition was confirmed in 2024, and a second edition has been in development since 2025. The practical consequence is clear: grading must be connected to product acceptance rules, not used as a generic seal.

### 4.5. Engineered bamboo products

The international field gained specific documents that were not available in Marçal's historical cut:

- [ISO 23478:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/75683.html): methods for physical and mechanical properties of prismatic forms of glued laminated bamboo and *scrimber*;
- [ISO 5257:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/81083.html): methods with small test specimens for defined engineered products, without establishing characteristic, design, or allowable values for a population;
- [ISO 7567:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/83076.html): product specifications for glued laminated bamboo structural elements.

These documents show both the progress of standardization and the limit of analogy. A conditioned and filled natural culm does not become glued laminated bamboo or *scrimber*. Methods from these documents can only be used when the object falls within scope or when adopted as an experimental reference with explicit justification, without declaring non-existent conformity.

## 5. The hybrid component and coverage gaps

### 5.1. One system, several objects of proof

The bamboo–PU–foam component must be decomposed into levels:

1. **incoming bamboo:** species, origin, geometry, moisture, defects, and grade;
2. **conditioning:** inputs, severity, waste, drying, and stability;
3. **external interface:** surface, adhesion, curing, continuity, and aging;
4. **internal filling:** density, voids, pressure, shrinkage, and adhesion;
5. **component:** stiffness, strength, deformation, and failure mode;
6. **connection:** load transfer, tolerances, and durability;
7. **subsystem:** wall, roof, truss, module, or assembled connection;
8. **building:** safety, fire, water, comfort, maintenance, and use.

Approval at a lower level does not replace the next level. PU property is not property of the coated culm; culm strength is not connection strength; connection strength is not building performance.

### 5.2. Design-by-testing requires a protocol prior to the result

ISO 22156 recognizes documented design-by-testing approaches within its scope. This does not mean testing a specimen until a favorable number is obtained. A design-by-testing program must define population, sampling, conditioning, variables, instrumentation, uncertainty, criteria, statistics, failure modes, and relationship to manufacturing.

For the hybrid system, the minimum controls include:

- untreated and unfilled culm;
- conditioned culm without PU;
- conditioned and coated culm;
- filled culm in the final configuration;
- a reference alternative pertinent to the function;
- aging compatible with the exposure class.

The test must separate material benefit, process effect, and geometry contribution.

### 5.3. Connections require their own validation

[Hong et al. (2019)](https://takwaratec.github.io/acervo-soberania-tecnologica/analyses/bambu-estrutural/china-bamboo-connections-review-2019/) show that bamboo connections present trade-offs between strength, stiffness, durability, workmanship, mass, and cost. No typology is universally superior.

A connection combining filling, adhesive, insert, or hardware requires tests representative of the load and use environment. The adhesive or foam data sheet does not provide the connection's load capacity. Nor is it enough to test the component without recording the failure mode: brittle failure, longitudinal splitting, pull-out, crushing, and slippage produce distinct risks.

## 6. Building performance and innovation assessment

### 6.1. The performance standard is not a material standard

The **ABNT NBR 15575 — Residential buildings — Performance** series organizes requirements for building behavior and its systems. Its parts undergo revisions at different times; the current edition of each must be consulted in the ABNT catalog before defining the program.

The use of bamboo, PU, or foam does not reduce the set of requirements. Depending on the subsystem and application, the following may be pertinent:

- structural safety;
- fire safety;
- watertightness;
- thermal and acoustic performance;
- durability and maintainability;
- safety in use and operation;
- functionality, accessibility, and comfort.

Meeting one structural requirement does not imply meeting thermal, acoustic, fire, or water requirements.

### 6.2. SiNAT for innovative construction products

The [Ministry of Cities](https://www.gov.br/cidades/pt-br/assuntos/habitacao/pbqp-h/sinat-sistema-nacional-de-avaliacoes-tecnicas-de-produtos-inovadores-e-sistemas-convencionais) defines SiNAT as the system for assessing innovative products without established Brazilian technical standards, and also conventional systems in relation to performance. The public route informs:

1. choice of an accredited Technical Assessment Institution;
2. contracting between proponent and institution;
3. technical assessment of the product;
4. submission of results to PBQP-H;
5. review and eventual issuance of DATec for innovation or FAD for conventional system;
6. publication of the document;
7. periodic renewal of the DATec.

The [official SiNAT FAQs](https://www.gov.br/mdr/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/perguntas-frequentes/perguntas-frequentes-habitacao/programa-brasileiro-da-qualidade-e-produtividade-do-habitat-pbqp-h/4-relativas-ao-sistema) distinguish two stages: design assessment and testing for the Technical Assessment Report, followed by audit of the quality control of the production process. The DATec has a defined validity and depends on keeping the product and process under control.

This route is especially relevant when the hybrid component becomes a construction system or subsystem intended for the housing market. It is not required for every laboratory research, but research must anticipate the data that a performance assessment will require.

> **Note on social housing financed with federal funds.** For social-interest housing covered by federal programs — such as Minha Casa Minha Vida and Caixa Econômica Federal financing lines — the adoption of innovative construction systems may be conditioned on the existence of a valid DATec or equivalent document issued by a PBQP-H Technical Assessment Institution. In this context, the DATec ceases to be optional and becomes part of the proposal's eligibility. Research and prototypes aiming at this market should plan the SiNAT route from the beginning of the experimental program, incorporating the data and controls that the assessment will require.

### 6.3. An assessed product is not a generic technology

A DATec, when issued, belongs to the configuration, process, proponent, and conditions described. Change of formulation, geometry, supplier, equipment, or control may require a new assessment. The distributed manufacturing proposed in Notebook 3 increases the importance of change control and equivalence among production units.

## 7. Fire, water, durability, and maintenance

### 7.1. UL 94 does not classify the building

The [certificate LCP10-000227](https://takwaratec.github.io/acervo-soberania-tecnologica/analyses/pu-vegetal/certificado-ul94-imperveg-2010/) assigns UL 94 V-2 classification to the identified polymeric sample. That result belongs to the sample and the method. It does not demonstrate fire resistance, surface spread, smoke, dripping, or the performance of the bamboo–PU–foam component in a building.

The correct use of the certificate is documentary: confirm identity, formulation, batch, and method, and decide which fire questions remain open.

### 7.2. Reaction-to-fire tests answer different questions

[ISO 11925-2:2026](https://www.iso.org/standard/89109.html) specifies an ignitability test by small flame on a vertical test specimen. [ISO 5660-1:2015](https://www.iso.org/standard/57957.html), with an amendment published in 2019, measures heat release rate and dynamic smoke production by the cone calorimeter method under defined conditions.

In the Brazilian context, **ABNT NBR 9442** — *Building materials — Determination of the surface flame spread index by the radiant panel method* — is the reference method for classifying internal and external lining materials in buildings. Its application to the bamboo–PU–foam hybrid component must be assessed together with the standard's scope and the geometry of the tested subsystem. The UL 94 V-2 test certificate available for the isolated polymer (IMPERVEG UG 132 A) does not replace the NBR 9442 classification of the final component.

These methods are not interchangeable with each other or with the ISO methods. Nor do they constitute, in isolation, a Brazilian use classification. The fire plan must start from the component, orientation, thickness, joints, fixings, coating, cavity, foam, and regulatory scenario.

> **State Fire Department requirements.** Fire Department technical regulations vary by state. In São Paulo, for example, the CBPMESP technical instruction pertinent to the occupancy defines the accepted tests and classifications for linings and construction components. Before starting the fire program, it is mandatory to consult the applicable IT for the intended occupancy, state, and building size. Conformity with ISO 5660-1 or ISO 11925-2 may not be sufficient, or even directly accepted as equivalent.

### 7.3. Watertightness is not nominal impermeability

Notebook 2 showed that coating, fiber, and voids form a permeable system whose response depends on manufacturing and aging. The product data sheet does not replace component tests at ends, nodes, cuts, perforations, and joints.

The program must distinguish:

- water absorption of the material;
- water passage through the component;
- vapor permeability;
- adhesion after cycles;
- cracking and film maintenance;
- water trapped inside the culm;
- watertightness of the assembled subsystem.

### 7.4. Durability is compatibility between exposure and maintenance

There is no demonstrated service life without exposure class, degradation mechanism, and maintenance plan. Biological treatment, coating, and foam can increase protection in one mechanism and create inspection difficulty in another.

The dossier must record foreseen agents, aging methods, inspections, repairs, replaceable parts, end-of-life criteria, and failure consequences. The release must state where the component cannot be used.

## 8. Technical responsibility

### 8.1. Responsibility cannot be attributed to the repository

The Acervo, an article, an artificial intelligence, or a scientific record does not assume responsibility for design or construction. [Law No. 6,496/1977](https://normativos.confea.org.br/Ementas/Visualizar?id=28) institutes the ART and defines it as the instrument that identifies, for legal purposes, those responsible for engineering works and services. [Law No. 12,378/2010](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2007-2010/2010/lei/l12378.htm) establishes the RRT for Architecture and Urbanism works and identifies authorship and responsibility within professional attributions.

The applicable document depends on the activity and the attributions of whoever assumes it. ART and RRT are not performance seals; they record responsibility for professional scopes. The responsibility network must cover, as applicable:

- conception and specification;
- characterization and testing;
- calculation and detailing;
- manufacturing and control;
- execution and assembly;
- inspection, maintenance, and alterations;
- technical assessment of the innovative product.

### 8.2. Community approval does not transfer technical risk

Participation and consent are indispensable but do not replace professional responsibility in regulated activity. Likewise, the presence of a professional does not replace participatory decision. Social governance and technical safety must coexist without one being used to legitimize the absence of the other.

## 9. Application–requirement–document–test–evidence–responsibility matrix

| Object and application | Requirement | Starting document | Necessary evidence | Responsibility to define |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Received culm | identity, moisture, geometry, and defects | ABNT NBR 16828; ISO 19624; supply plan | batch record, measurement, and grading | supplier, incoming control |
| Culm for structural design | characteristic properties | ABNT NBR 16828-2; ISO 22157 | sampling, testing, and statistical analysis | laboratory and designer |
| Conditioned culm | integrity and stability | own protocol + pertinent methods | before/after comparison, pH, moisture, strength, and traceability | process responsible and laboratory |
| Bamboo–PU interface | adhesion and durability | selected and justified adhesion method | failure mode, aging, and variability | specifier, laboratory, and manufacturer |
| Foam inside the culm | filling and mechanical effect | own protocol guided by function | density, voids, pressure, shrinkage, load, and failure | manufacturer, laboratory, and designer |
| Hybrid connection | strength, stiffness, and ductility | pertinent connection standard/method + design-by-testing | representative series, conditioning, and rupture mode | designer and laboratory |
| Structural component | limit states and durability | ABNT NBR 16828-1; ISO 22156 within scope | calculation, properties, tests, and safety factor | qualified professional |
| Defined engineered product | properties and specification | ISO 23478, ISO 5257, or ISO 7567 as per scope | tests and control of the covered product | manufacturer, laboratory, and designer |
| Residential wall or roof | performance in use | current ABNT NBR 15575 series | structural, fire, water, thermal, acoustic, and durability as per function | designers, manufacturer, and assessor |
| Innovative construction product | harmonized assessment | SiNAT and applicable guideline | RTA, production audit, and eventual DATec | proponent and ITA |
| Reaction to fire | ignitability, heat, and smoke | required Brazilian methods; ISO 11925-2/5660-1 as references when accepted | test specimens in pertinent configuration | specialist and accredited/accepted laboratory |
| Distributed production | equivalence among units | control plan and technical assessment | audits, calibration, witness samples, and interlaboratory comparison | system holder and producing units |
| Delivered building | use, maintenance, and alterations | standards, project, manual, and local legislation | inspection, records, and post-occupancy assessment | designers, builders, managers, and users as per scope |

This matrix guides planning. The final applicable document must be confirmed by a qualified professional, laboratory, and competent authority.

## 10. Eight release gates

### Gate 0 — use, territorial coherence, and regulatory environment

Define function, occupancy, failure consequence, location, exposure, service life, and competent authority. Confirm that the problem and demand were diagnosed, that proportional capacity exists to operate, measure, maintain, and dispose of waste, and that the alternative can be refused without transferring harm to the community. Map standards, regulations, licensing, responsibilities, and continuity sources. The detailed diagnosis belongs to Notebooks 3 and 5.

**Output:** statement of intended use, record of socio-territorial coherence, and versioned normative map.

### Gate 1 — identity and specification of inputs

Define bamboo, PU, foam, hardware, additives, and treatments. Record suppliers, batches, critical properties, storage, validity, and permitted substitutions.

**Output:** input specifications and rejection criteria.

### Gate 2 — stable process

Characterize operations, instruments, tolerances, safety, waste, maintenance, and operator qualification. Produce repeated batches before qualification testing.

**Output:** versioned procedure and preliminary repeatability evidence.

### Gate 3 — material and interface

Test bamboo properties, conditioning effects, curing, adhesion, filling, and aging. Separate controls and record failure mode.

**Output:** technical report identifying properties and uncertainties.

### Gate 4 — component and connection

Test representative size and configuration under relevant actions. Include nodes, ends, tolerances, and permitted defects. Compare empty and filled when the foam function is at stake.

**Output:** characteristic values or capacity criteria defined by the accepted method, without undue extrapolation.

### Gate 5 — subsystem and exposure

Assess fire, water, heat, sound, aging, maintenance, and interaction with other components as per use. Test joints, passages, and singular points.

**Output:** performance matrix, use restrictions, and maintenance plan.

### Gate 6 — external assessment and production control

When applicable, contract assessment by a competent institution, execute the required program, and audit manufacturing. Handle non-conformities and changes.

**Output:** assessment, certification, or specific technical document — only when actually issued.

### Gate 7 — release and monitoring

Release only the version, plant, batch, application, and exposure covered. Deliver manual, identification, inspection, and occurrence channel. Monitor first uses and suspend upon critical failure.

**Output:** delimited release statement, signed by those responsible, with validity and version control.

## 11. Minimum conformity dossier

Each product or component must have:

1. identity, ownership, and version;
2. intended use and prohibited uses;
3. controlled drawings and materials list;
4. applicable standards and regulations with edition and consultation date;
5. risk analysis and failure modes;
6. input specifications;
7. manufacturing and safety procedures;
8. inspection, measurement, and testing plan;
9. calibration and competence of those who execute;
10. complete reports, raw data, and uncertainties;
11. acceptance, rework, and disposal criteria;
12. batch traceability and witness specimens;
13. durability, fire, water, and maintenance assessment as per use;
14. technical responsibility records;
15. external assessment or DATec when required;
16. use, inspection, repair, and end-of-life manual;
17. change history and revalidation;
18. field records and occurrence handling.

The dossier may have public and protected layers. Technical secrecy cannot hide from assessors the information necessary for safety.

## 12. Change control

In a hybrid system, small changes can alter performance:

- bamboo species or origin;
- moisture range and geometry;
- polymer formulation or supplier;
- foam density and expansion;
- treatment, drying, and storage;
- application and curing method;
- hardware, insert, or connection geometry;
- equipment, operator, or production unit;
- finish, exposure, or maintenance.

Each change must be classified as editorial, process, or design. A process or design change requires impact analysis and may require retesting. One must not keep a version number, certificate, or claim after modifying the object that originated them.

## 13. What the evidence allows — and what it still does not allow

### 13.1. Supported claims

- There are specific Brazilian and international standards for design and characterization of structures with culms.
- Bamboo grading, property testing, and design are distinct documentary functions.
- ISO 22156:2021 does not cover engineered products nor all hybrid materials with bamboo.
- There are ISO standards specific to certain engineered bamboo products.
- An innovative construction product without sufficient prescriptive coverage may require performance assessment through SiNAT.
- Innovation assessment includes production control, not only initial testing.
- A certificate for an isolated polymer does not classify a component or building.
- ART or RRT identify professional responsibilities but do not replace demonstrated performance.
- It is possible to structure a conformity program by layers and gates.

### 13.2. Active conformity criteria

| Claim or decision | Corresponding evidence/document | Block while absent |
|---|---|---|
| apply a bamboo standard to the hybrid component | explicit verification of scope and coverage of object, process, and use | the mere presence of bamboo does not transfer conformity |
| preserve properties after treatment/filling | before/after characterization, controls, aging, and variability | using original culm properties as if they were the component's |
| approve by a test standard | requirement or acceptance criterion defined by project, regulation, or competent assessment | confusing measurement method with approval |
| use ISO in Brazilian context | compatibility confirmed with standards, regulations, and competent authority | unilateral substitution of national/local requirement |
| declare fire safety | method and configuration pertinent to component, subsystem, and occupancy | transferring UL 94 or input certificate to the building |
| declare adhesion, impermeability, or reinforcement | test of property, configuration, aging, and failure mode | using commercial data sheet as proof of the assembly |
| repeat production | stable process, control plan, traceability, audit, and change handling | generalizing one favorable test to future batches |
| release critical application | all pertinent gates completed and responsibilities formalized | presenting the authorial system as homologated |

Prototype, patent, DOI, ART, RRT, certificate, test report, and DATec remain distinct documents. Each one answers only for the scope it actually records.

## 14. Limitations

- The review does not reproduce protected full texts of standards.
- The normative catalog changes and must be checked again for each project.
- The notebook does not analyze the requirements of a specific municipality, state, or Fire Department.
- The ABNT NBR 15575 series and complementary standards must be selected according to the subsystem and occupancy.
- No single standard was found for the natural conditioned, coated, and filled culm in the authorial configuration.
- The final selection of methods depends on the laboratory, assessor, and qualified professional.
- Contractual, certification, intellectual property, and product regulation matters require their own specialized analysis.

## 15. Conclusion

The prototype demonstrates that a configuration can be built. Conformity requires demonstrating that an identified version meets defined requirements, under a controlled process, for delimited application and exposure. Between these two states there is a chain of decisions that no single standard replaces.

The normative map confirms that there is a solid base for structural culms and a growing international field for engineered bamboo products. It also confirms a gap: the bamboo–PU–foam hybrid component crosses different scopes and must be assessed as a system of interfaces. Using a standard outside its object can produce the appearance of rigor without corresponding safety.

The contribution of this notebook is to convert standardization into a working method. Each claim must carry six answers: **which object, which requirement, which document, which evidence, which limit, and who is responsible**. When one of these answers is missing, the claim remains in research.

The recommended next technical step is to choose a single component and intended use, hold a scoping meeting with a qualified professional and a Technical Assessment Institution or pertinent laboratory, and produce the specific normative map before commissioning the testing program. Only then will it be possible to estimate cost, schedule, and the real release route.
